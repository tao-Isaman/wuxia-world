Wave 9 independent whole-game opening critique — 2026-09-29

The reviewed build is playable through the requested opening and investigation. It does **not** reach the visual or dramatic standard of Hero's Adventure: Road to Passion or Dokapon Kingdom Connect. I did not have a sustained “wow” response. The backgrounds are attractive, but attractive scenery and successful quest flags do not yet make the people and events feel convincingly present in that scenery.

The single biggest remaining gap is **how characters and events are staged in the world**. The game still often reads as a richly detailed picture with small, separately styled actors and menu interactions over it. That gap is more consequential than adding another quest, another reward card, or more background detail.

This review covers production at `http://127.0.0.1:3017` after the root's explicit GO and before my `BROWSER_CLOSED` notification. Later edits are outside this verdict. I read the mandatory coordination documents and the official reference manifests/images, but no product source, earlier review reports, or other-agent status. The mandatory shared document contained historical implementation summaries, and both reference games are identifiable. This is an independent output/play review, **not a scientifically blind experiment**.

I used one active Chromium browser at a time, controlled with a Node/Playwright driver, with no video recording. A launch permission failure and closed stdin required one restart before creating the character. Actual play used one fresh character, River, default male appearance and base attributes, ordinary UI actions, and unedited saves, inventory, stats, quest state and RNG. The first random herb find, later enemy encounter and later potion find were accepted as rolled. No console/store calls were used to advance gameplay. Desktop was 1440×900; smaller checks used 390×844 and 844×390 in the same browser. One portrait ground move used emulated touch events; most menu actions used normal pointer clicks. This does not establish performance or touch reliability on physical phones.

I encountered **no progression blocker in these flows**. The conversation exits I actually used worked, including leaving the ledger chain midway and resuming it. This is bounded coverage, not a claim that every conversation, map, quest or battle works. The reload check was within the same browser context, not a browser/OS restart. No unhandled `pageerror` was captured; no broad performance profiling was performed.

Three issues still matter to operation and presentation:

1. At 844×390, the doctor's dialogue initially shows prose with the first choice clipped at the panel's bottom and the departure choice below it. Manual scrolling reveals both. This is recoverable, but a player can read it as a missing choice because the overflow cue is weak. A persistent visible choice area or clear continuation/scroll indicator would help. The before/after screenshots above demonstrate this without relying on Playwright's automatic scroll into view.
2. The palace destination says “พักในลานวัง” (rest in the palace courtyard), then opens choices labelled “พักที่วัด” (rest at a temple) and roadside rest. The action works, but the location fiction changes inside the modal. Both travel exits are correctly distinguished and functionally verified.
3. Routine dialogue completion can abruptly interrupt the scene with an unrelated random event. A herb find followed accepting the clinic task, and an “evil sect leader” encounter followed claiming its reward; I fled safely. The reward then appeared back in the capital. This did not cost progress, but it weakened the sense of a continuous authored exchange. Choice numbering also exposes gaps such as 1/2/5 and a lone 5, and Wu's success receipt repeats conditional pre-handoff dialogue rather than acknowledging the hand-in.

The palace capture further exposes the staging problem: a large decorated courtyard has no visible social activity, only the player and interaction symbols. At Yuelai Inn, the blue-robed NPC and the player's default appearance visibly repeat the same hero design side by side. These are direct observations from [palace](wave9-evidence/28-palace-desktop.png) and [inn](wave9-evidence/32-palace-exit-inn.png), not inferences from source. A brief keyboard walk toward the capital's southern shop stopped at its front, and portrait movement routed through the street; that limited sample does not validate collision throughout the world.

The most valuable next change is a focused scene-staging pass: give the next important exchange a consistent actor scale and distinct identity, focus the camera on its participants, and make the manipulated prop visibly change at the moment of interaction. Judge that pass in motion and beside the same references. More text and larger scenic images alone will not close the gap.
