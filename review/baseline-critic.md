# Baseline game-quality review — direct, named comparison

**Observed:** 28 September 2026, 20:48:15–20:48:41 Asia/Bangkok (13:48 UTC), Chromium at `http://127.0.0.1:3017`. This is the production baseline captured before the parent restarted the server for renderer work. It is not a verdict on subsequent edits.

**Verdict:** Attractive illustrated assets and a working interaction loop, but the playable presentation is still visibly a prototype beside either reference. The primary shortfall is that the character does not convincingly inhabit or act within the environment. Increasing texture detail alone will not resolve it.

## Evidence and limits

I created a fresh character, moved with D, opened Destinations, selected the home exit, followed the route destination, selected a combat skill, observed damage and victory, tapped to move at 390×844, and resized that session to 844×390. The route was still in progress when combat was seeded; I did not finish a capital/NPC interaction. Combat used the permitted test's `petty_thief` save setup with STR 25 and AGI 10, so its one-hit victory says nothing about normal balance or tactical depth. This also explains the artificial 36/108 player HP display.

Screenshots and recordings are in [baseline/](baseline/). [observations.json](baseline/observations.json) contains capture timestamps and the visible DOM text. [run-baseline.cjs](run-baseline.cjs) records the precise actions. App/test implementation was not changed. Only browser-test navigation, Playwright configuration, and mandatory coordination files were read; the shared coordination file contained prior implementation notes, which were not treated as visual evidence. No design document or renderer implementation was consulted.

This was a named visual comparison, not an identity-hidden test. I inspected actual official Steam screenshot image files, not just the store descriptions. I did not play either reference, so I do not claim comparative balance, responsiveness, animation smoothness, or complete gameplay parity.

## Five independently judgeable gaps

| 1 | **Actor/environment integration — visual blocker for the stated quality target.** The tall, sharply outlined hero renders over the gate roof and overlaps the well's upper structure. The scene uses an elevated viewpoint but the hero presents a nearly frontal standing illustration. Detailed cloth is visible; convincing placement is not. Actor scale/perspective/shadow should remain coherent throughout the crossing. |
| 2 | **Movement and attack acting are weak — visual blocker.** In the sampled movement sequence, the silhouette retains the same arms/legs while translating and flipping. There is no clearly readable stepping cycle. Combat does have visible feedback: a skill name, floating damage, opponent flash/fade, and victory. But the demonstrated punch is carried mainly by effects rather than a clearly distinct attacking body pose. See [movement frames](baseline/movement-frames.jpg), [battle frames](baseline/battle-frames.jpg), and [impact](baseline/battle-action-0.png). | A viewer must distinguish idle, walking, windup, contact, recoil, and recovery from the characters alone. Validate at normal playback speed; a changed transform is not adequate evidence of articulated movement. |
| 3 | **The first minute lacks a clear purpose — onboarding gap.** The home screen explains movement and interaction keys, then presents eight equally weighted systems. There is no visible first objective or obvious person/activity to approach. The resting icon looks like an opaque square sticker; the exit is below the initial desktop framing. Destinations makes navigation possible, but initially lists activities without explaining why to choose one. See [first world screen](baseline/world-desktop.png) and [destinations](baseline/world-destinations.png). | A fresh player should be able to name their immediate objective, identify its location, and complete the first useful action without opening every bottom menu or relying on test selectors. |
| 4 | **Battle state/choice hierarchy is too small — communication gap.** At 1440×900, two large status boxes precede a roughly 710×400 arena in a centered panel. The actual choice is one fist icon beneath small instruction text, with no persistent visible move name or cost. The ATB wait is understandable after reading the small status line, but the transition into player control is understated. The pale victory box is visually disconnected from the jade/gold scene. See [waiting](baseline/battle-desktop.png), [skill choice extracted from recording](baseline/battle-choice.png), and [result](baseline/battle-after-action.png). | Clearly announce whose action is available, keep the move name and relevant cost/effect visible, and preserve a stable composition through wait/choice/impact/result. Mobile must retain this information without relying on hover. |
| 5 | **Landscape resize breaks the observed viewport — functional blocker requiring reproduction.** At 390×844 portrait the game fits, though HUD panels and tiny bottom labels compete with the play area. After resizing to 844×390 and waiting 500 ms, scenery occupies only the left 390 px; the right side is solid green and the hero is below the viewport. Touch instructions also become keyboard instructions. See [portrait](baseline/world-mobile.png) and [landscape](baseline/world-landscape.png). This is one observed resize, not a claim about every device or a permanent failure. | Rotate a real touch session in both directions, wait through resize completion, and verify the entire viewport renders, the hero stays visible, touch guidance remains appropriate, and every primary action remains reachable. |

**Single biggest gap:** actor/environment integration. The gate-crossing image alone visibly contradicts the apparent depth of the world. Fix perspective, contact with the ground, occlusion, and believable movement before adding more decorative detail.

## What the reference images actually demonstrate

- **Hero's Adventure:** [official store](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1948980/Heros_Adventure_Road_to_Passion/), inspected screenshots [village](baseline/ref-1948980-0.jpg), [night scene](baseline/ref-1948980-1.jpg), [panda scene](baseline/ref-1948980-2.jpg), and [combat](baseline/ref-1948980-3.jpg). - **Dokapon Kingdom: Connect:** [official store](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2338140/Dokapon_Kingdom_Connect/) and [official site](https://dokaponkingdom.com/). ## Blockers versus taste

The landscape failure and incorrect depth ordering are observable defects. Weak motion/state communication and first-action guidance materially impede the intended experience. Palette, adult proportions versus chibi characters, a muted interface versus Dokapon's bright UI, and painterly texture versus clean pixel outlines are taste choices. The existing title composition, clothing detail, environmental texture, Thai labels, keyboard/touch movement, and visible damage/result feedback are useful foundations. None of those strengths establishes commercial-reference gameplay parity.
