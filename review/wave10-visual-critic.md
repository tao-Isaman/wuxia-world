## Static verdict

The production has attractive, richly detailed locations and usable small-screen staging. It does not yet match either reference's character presentation and cohesive finish. The biggest discrepancy is the division between scenic detail and actor detail: the scenery can carry a close look, while large combat actors become soft clusters at faces, hands, and cloth edges. More background detail would not resolve that discrepancy.

## Reference limits and priorities

The reference sets are marketing stills, not recordings of play. I do not infer their input quality or animation smoothness. They are not flawless: `ref-1948980-5.jpg` has awkwardly wrapped lower UI labels. Nor does Dokapon's bright 3D style need to be copied into a Thai wuxia pixel-art game. The useful quality bar is readable intent, consistent drawing conventions, expressive character focus, and a world organized around the current moment.

The three highest-value visual improvements suggested by the inspected output are (1) bring actor face/hand/edge quality into agreement with scenery at their actual display scale, (2) frame conversation portraits around expressive faces rather than tiny full figures, and (3) give battle turns and important dialogue outcomes stronger moment-specific emphasis. I will refine action-related findings only after observing ordinary live play.

## Neutral candidate A/B

**Recorded preference: B, for the displayed base-pose character at actual game scale.** This verdict was written before reading any candidate mapping or provenance; neither `mapping.json` nor `rendered-report.json` was opened. No candidate identity is needed to explain the judgment.

Evidence directly viewed: `review/m1-readability-v2/comparison.png`, `A-idle-world.png`, `B-idle-world.png`, `A-attack-world.png`, `B-attack-world.png`, and original-scale pairs `A/B-pose-00.png`, `A/B-pose-09.png`, `A/B-walk-4.png`, `A/B-walk-7.png`. The comparison presents the full 16 base-pose strip, four runtime walking captures, and full capital context. I checked selected 128×128 crops separately at their original scale instead of relying only on the overview.

- **Why B:** The larger head, clear skin area, heavier grouping of the hair and robe, and visible forward hand make face direction and intent legible. In `B-pose-09.png`, the fist is a distinct shape beyond the sleeve and torso; in the matching A crop, the narrow arm/hand is much easier to lose against the wall. B's hit/guard/victory/defeat strip is also easier to categorize immediately because the limbs and head occupy clearer shapes. Its blue clothing reads as one designed silhouette rather than several small highlights.
- **What A does better:** Its longer body and finer robe retain a more restrained wuxia illustration style and less exaggerated anatomy. At the inspected world size that refinement is too small to repay the lost face/hand readability. More nominal detail is not producing more useful detail.
- **Walking evidence:** The `walk-4` through `walk-7` row is labeled as actual keyboard/runtime movement; changing screen position and leg poses are visible. B gives stronger leg separation and a clearer head/torso shape in those captures. These are still frames, so this is a judgment of motion poses, not smoothness, foot sliding, cadence, or temporal consistency.
- **Pose evidence:** Idle, walking-at-fixed-position, attack, and hit/guard/victory/defeat strips are forced engine frames for art inspection. I do not describe the diagnostic punch as a played combat action.
- **Largest remaining gap:** B improves the protagonist as a readable game token, but a single stylized hero against this intricately textured town does not establish a unified cast. Its enlarged head and cleaner shapes would need agreement with NPC scale/proportions, dialogue portraits, combat enlargement, and all directional poses. The neutral diagnostic intentionally omits NPC/UI layers and does not validate the supplemental north/south sheet. I therefore support B's readability direction, not an unconditional whole-game replacement or commercial-quality parity claim.

Against Hero's Adventure, B moves closer to the useful principle visible in `ref-1948980-0.jpg` and `-6.jpg`: allocate enough shape and face area to read the actor immediately. It does not require copying that game's chibi proportions exactly. Against Dokapon, the sampled pose design still lacks the dramatic facial/gesture focus of `ref-2338140-5.jpg` and `-7.jpg`; a clearer idle sprite alone cannot supply that presentation.
