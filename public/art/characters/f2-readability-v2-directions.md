## Exact generation prompt

Use case: identity-preserve.
Asset type: transparent NORTH/SOUTH four-frame walking supplements for one original adult female wuxia pixel-art game hero, consistent with her compact sixteen-pose base sheet.
Input image 1: ORIGINAL directions sheet, reference for the woman's exact identity, costume/back details, and the row motion order ONLY. Input image 2: her NEW compact base sheet, authoritative reference for larger visible skull/face, enlarged hands, shorter adult body, face design, costume colors and pixel technique. Input image 3: accepted compact cobalt male candidate, reference for overall proportional readability ONLY. Never turn the woman into the male hero or copy his blue costume.
IDENTITY: Adult athletic scarlet martial artist: dark-brown high ponytail/topknot with long bright-red ribbon tails, assertive warm mature feminine face; vermilion short-sleeve crossed tunic with cream collar and narrow gold edging, red knot sash, long split scarlet coat tails over an ivory short pleated underlayer, bare lower arms and upper legs, white cloth hand/wrist wraps, brown-red lace-up calf boots. She fights unarmed; no sword or bow.
Primary request: draw exactly EIGHT complete separate full-body walking frames of this same woman on one TRUE ALPHA TRANSPARENT PNG. Four columns by TWO rows, equal consistent cells. Row 1 faces directly AWAY from viewer and walks NORTH, showing the back of head/hair, clothing and stowed equipment. Row 2 faces TOWARD viewer and walks SOUTH, clearly showing the SAME mature face from input 2. No side-facing frames, extra idle row, titles or labels.
Each row left-to-right is a four-step cycle: left foot forward/right back, passing contact, right foot forward/left back, opposite passing contact. Counter-swing arms naturally. The rear boot sole is visible only in the appropriate back-view lift frames. Hair, sleeve and coat tails move consistently with gait. Maintain same head size, torso length and body scale in all eight frames, and match compact base sheet input 2. Do not redraw input 1's long legs and tiny face.
Proportion/style: top-of-skull-to-soles about FOUR AND A HALF head lengths. Adult woman with a defined small nose and brow, warm clear face and hand pixel planes; no infant/chibi mascot or oversized round eyes. Crisp stepped contours and square pixel clusters, detailed 32-bit-era authored sprite craft, slim dark outline, simplified light/midtone/shadow fabric planes and selective pale edge pixels. No busy fabric speckles, smooth gradients, painted antialiasing, blur, aura, neon fringe or decorative noise.
Layout is crucial: roomy equal four-column/two-row grid, each entire pose inside the middle 80 percent of its cell with clear TRANSPARENT gutters and generous outer margins. Feet around 86 percent of each cell height. Every foot, hair tip, sleeve, bow and sword complete and fully visible. At least 24 source pixels of empty transparency between rows and no touching silhouettes. Weapons stay stowed, and all original equipment stays present when visible from that direction.
True transparent alpha background, not a black panel, checkerboard, colored gradient or scenic backdrop. No floor, cast shadow, effects, grid lines, text, labels, borders or unrelated objects. This is original game art, not a commercial sprite copy.
